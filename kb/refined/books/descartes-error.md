We have always ranked reasoning above feeling, as the higher faculty. Damasio shows, through patients who lost emotional perception, that reason on its own cannot act: they understood the right choice perfectly and still could not make it. Logic turns out to be a tool; what actually moves us to change is the chemistry we experience as emotion. I read this early, and it planted the view of the brain I still hold — see "Chemistry is the optimizer."
