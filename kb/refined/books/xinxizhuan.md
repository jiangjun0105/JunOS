Written in Chinese by Dr. Wu Jun, it covers information theory — entropy, how to measure information, information density — and how it changed the way we design machine learning and all kinds of algorithms. It gave me the gist of information theory.

More than that, it added a new layer to how I analyze problems from first principles, as fundamental as knowing physics or mathematics. Seen through it, backprop and a brain both move information from data into a network, but backprop updates every weight globally and a brain updates a small local area; that's why I think backprop can't match biological learning efficiency. More on that in Thoughts.
