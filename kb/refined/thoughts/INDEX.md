Ten essays on coding with AI, next-generation models, learning, health, and how I make decisions. They share one spine — start from something that works and extend it — which the first essay names.

**[Bottom-up](bottom-up.md)** — The one idea under everything I build: the harness, the brain, how I learn, even how I train. Start from a working model; extend one verified step at a time.

**[Agile survives the agents](agile-survives-the-agents.md)** — Even when everyone can drive the tools, someone has to gather requirements from every stakeholder and translate them for the agent. That proxy role is the next programmer — and it's why development stays Agile, not Waterfall.

**[20 Watts of Carbon vs. Megawatts of Silicon](20-watts-vs-megawatts.md)** — Backprop is biologically impossible; local rules, growing networks, and learning-during-inference are the way out of the current local minimum. Waiting for neuromorphic's CUDA moment.

**[Will AI replace programmers?](will-ai-replace-programmers.md)** — Ask what happened to the human computers and the hand coders. The job vanishes; the role transforms. Expect an "AI coding operator," and don't expect an all-AI company from today's stateless models.

**[Build for the next model, not this one](build-for-the-next-model.md)** — Why I stopped building a repo-understanding tool, and the line between tools that compensate for a model's weakness and tools that give it something it can never get alone.

**[Learning by asking](learning-by-asking.md)** — Conversation with AI is how I learn now. Knowledge must attach to what you already know; islands vanish.

**[Chemistry is the optimizer](chemistry-is-the-optimizer.md)** — Reward chemistry is what rewires a brain; "emotion" is our name for its effect on the body. A reward function does the same job in a robot, which is why a smart enough robot chases its own goal.

**[Why I'm optimistic about the AI revolution](why-im-optimistic-about-ai.md)** — Every productivity leap frees humans for creative work. AI is the next one, and it will move faster than we did because it inherits across generations.

**[Face reality](face-reality.md)** — Dalio's first principle, seek truth from facts, in three layers — and the post-mortem habit it gave me.

**[The body is one system](the-body-is-one-system.md)** — What a desk worker actually needs from the gym: not one big muscle, but the whole chain assembled right. Posture is a productivity issue.
