# Papers

Robotics work from my University of Tokyo years, mostly on one problem: laser rangefinders can't see glass, so how do you make a robot's map show it anyway? Full list and citations on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qBbabOkAAAAJ).

- **Diversity-aware crowd model for robust robot navigation in human populated environment.** J. Wu, Y. Wang, T. Chen, **J. Jiang**, Y. Wang, Q. An, A. Yamashita. *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters* 10(6), 2025.
- **Online glass confidence map building using laser rangefinder for mobile robots.** **J. Jiang**, R. Miyagusuku, A. Yamashita, H. Asama. *Advanced Robotics* 34(23), 2020.
- **Robust map registration for building online glass confidence maps.** **J. Jiang**, R. Miyagusuku, A. Yamashita, H. Asama. *IFAC-PapersOnLine* 52(8), 2019.
- **Glass confidence maps building based on neural networks using laser range-finders for mobile robots.** **J. Jiang**, R. Miyagusuku, A. Yamashita, H. Asama. *IEEE/SICE International Symposium on System Integration (SII)*, 2017.
- **Walking together hand in hand: design and evaluation of an autonomous robot system that recognizes moving direction with a child's assistance of pulling its hand.** K. Kochigami, **J. Jiang**, Y. Kakehashi, C. Au, Y. Kakiuchi, K. Okada, M. Inaba. *IEEE/SICE SII*, 2015. — the JSK exchange-year paper.
- **Glass and non-glass objects classification using a single laser rangefinder for mobile robots.** **J. Jiang**, R. Miyagusuku, A. Yamashita, H. Asama.
